Water-Saving Substrate Planning for Arid Greenhouses

How molded substrate structure can support water-conscious greenhouse programs.

June 5, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

In arid climates, substrate structure, irrigation frequency and packaging cleanliness all matter. MCCS molded substrate plugs are designed for controlled hydration and can be tested in buyer-side growing conditions before bulk planning.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm application and target tray or holder.
  • Request wet-size data and sample testing before bulk order.
  • Clarify packaging format, carton quantity and monthly volume.
  • Discuss SGS test report and export documents if required by your market.
