## Partner Link Targets

Approach existing or active commercial relationships first:

- Propagation-tray suppliers: request a compatibility-resource link after a documented fit trial.
- Automated transplanter integrators: offer the neutral staged trial protocol as a customer resource.
- Saudi greenhouse integrators and distributors: link to the local sample and trial protocol after confirming availability.
- Private-label packaging partners: co-author a product-plus-pack approval checklist.
- Research and grower partners: request a source citation only when they have reviewed the referenced method or trial context.

Do not exchange links at scale, buy directory links or imply that a partner tested MCCS when no documented test exists.
