Prioritize commercial greenhouse operators, hydroponic system brands, nursery suppliers, propagation tray suppliers, orchid nurseries, tissue culture labs, grow kit brands, garden-center private-label buyers, and horticulture distributors.

## Weekly rhythm

Monday: find 50 prospects and select 20 A/B-fit buyers.
Tuesday: send 20 customized cold emails.
Wednesday: add 20 LinkedIn procurement or product contacts.
Thursday: follow up with previous-week non-replies.
Friday: publish or update one SEO page and check Search Console.
Saturday: turn inquiry questions into FAQ and article topics.
