# Weekly GSC Growth Workflow

Use this workflow once per week after the new SEO pages are deployed. The purpose is to improve pages that already show evidence of search demand, not to resubmit unchanged URLs.

## Weekly Comparison

Compare the latest 7 days with the previous 7 days in Google Search Console.

Record:

- Total clicks
- Total impressions
- Average CTR
- Average position
- Top queries
- Top pages
- Country results for the United States, Canada, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates
- Device split

## Prioritization Rules

### Improve the search snippet

Review the SEO title, meta description and opening paragraph when a page has:

- At least 20 impressions in the comparison period
- An average position between 4 and 20
- Zero clicks or CTR materially below the site average

The new title and description must match the visible page. Do not add unsupported performance claims.

### Strengthen content and internal links

Expand a page when GSC shows relevant queries that the page only answers partially. Add the missing buyer question, method or evidence to the existing page rather than creating a near-duplicate page.

Add two to four descriptive internal links from relevant indexed pages. Do not add sitewide exact-match keyword links.

### Wait without changing the page

Do not rewrite a page merely because it has not received clicks during its first few weeks. Wait when:

- Google has indexed the page but impressions are still low.
- The page was substantially updated during the previous 14 days.
- The query is relevant but the average position is still below 30.

### Request indexing only when justified

Use URL Inspection for:

- A newly deployed page
- A page with a substantial content or canonical correction
- A page that was excluded because of a technical error that has now been fixed

Do not repeatedly request indexing for unchanged pages.

## Indexing Checks

Review these separately from performance:

- Sitemap status remains successful.
- New application and protocol pages are discovered.
- Excluded pages are intentional redirects or canonical duplicates.
- Product structured-data errors decline after the product catalog is recrawled.

## Monthly Decision

At the end of four weekly cycles:

- Keep and strengthen pages that are gaining relevant impressions.
- Consolidate pages that compete for the same query and buyer intent.
- Create a new page only when GSC or sales conversations reveal a distinct unanswered procurement task.
- Use inquiry quality, not traffic alone, as the B2B success measure.
