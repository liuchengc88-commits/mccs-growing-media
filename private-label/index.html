B2B FAQ

Private Label FAQ

Can MCCS support buyer packaging before bulk orders?

Yes. Packaging format, carton markings, instruction cards and sample approval can be reviewed before a private label order moves forward.

Can I compare models before choosing packaging?

Yes. Start with product application and tray or container fit, then use the selected model to plan packaging format and sample evaluation.

Can export documents be coordinated with packaging plans?

Export coordination can be discussed alongside carton markings, buyer labels and shipping method after project details are confirmed.
