Shipping Options

Courier, Air or Sea Discussion Based on Your Order Stage

  • DHL / FedEx / UPS: suitable for first sample packs and urgent evaluation.
  • Air shipment: suitable for mid-volume trial orders and faster replenishment.
  • Sea shipment: suitable for private-label bulk orders and regular supply planning.
  • Documents: invoice, packing list and route-specific support can be discussed according to market needs.
