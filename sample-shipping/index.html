Sample policy: Small sample quantities may be free or low-cost depending on model and stock. International courier freight is normally paid by the buyer. Sample preparation usually takes 3–5 working days after confirmation, and courier delivery commonly takes 5–10 working days depending on destination.
Shipping Options

Courier, Air or Sea Planning by Order Stage

  • Courier: suitable for first sample packs and urgent evaluation.
  • Air shipment: suitable for mid-volume trial orders and faster replenishment.
  • Sea shipment: suitable for private-label bulk orders and recurring supply.
  • Documents: invoice, packing list and route-specific documents can be discussed by destination market.

Replacement policy

If the wrong model is packed or sample damage is caused by our packing error, we will discuss replacement in the next shipment or sample dispatch.

Return handling

Because growing media samples are low-value and international return freight is high, returns are normally handled through replacement or credit discussion rather than physical return.
