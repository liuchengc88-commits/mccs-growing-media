Sample Cost

Samples may be free or charged depending on model count, quantity and packaging request. Final sample cost is confirmed before shipment.

Freight Payment

Courier freight is normally paid by the buyer or shipped under the buyer’s courier account.

Timing Estimate

Sample preparation normally takes 3–5 working days. Courier delivery commonly takes 5–9 working days depending on destination and customs process.

Workflow

Sample-to-Bulk Route

A staged workflow keeps evaluation practical and reduces order risk.

1. Model shortlist

Select CF models by application and tray fit.

2. Sample pack

Verify size, hydration, handling and first packaging impression.

3. Trial order

Test real use, label options and carton configuration.

4. Bulk shipment

Confirm carton quantity, documents, shipping method and schedule.

Replacement Policy

Handling Sample Issues

If damage, model mismatch or serious packing issue is confirmed with clear photos, replacement pieces can be discussed or added to the next shipment. Normal performance testing results should be evaluated through repeat trials in the buyer’s actual tray or growing environment.

First sample route

Model confirmation → sample packing → courier booking → buyer testing feedback.

Bulk-shipment route

Packaging confirmation → carton quantity → shipping method → document preparation.
