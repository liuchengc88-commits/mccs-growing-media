Sample & Shipping

Sample Packs, Trial Orders and Export Shipping Support

A clear sample workflow helps buyers accelerate their sourcing decisions before trial packing and bulk shipment planning.

Request Quote & SampleEmail Sales

Sample Pack

20–50 pcs

Samples are usually charged at a small handling cost or deducted from a confirmed bulk order after approval.

Trial Pack

100–200 pcs

Trial packs help verify germination, handling, tray fit and short production performance before bulk orders.

Packaging Mockup

Retail box, refill pouch or label-ready carton

Mockups are quoted based on artwork, print method and packaging complexity.

Shipping note: Sample shipments do not include seeds, fertilizer, or restricted accessories unless separately confirmed.
Shipping Options

Courier, Air, or Sea — Options by Order Stage

  • Sample preparation: normally 3–5 working days after model and sample count are confirmed.
  • Courier delivery: usually 5–9 working days depending on country, customs and courier network.
  • Freight policy: sample freight is normally paid by the buyer or shipped under the buyer’s courier account.
  • Replacement handling: if sample damage or model mismatch is confirmed with photos, we can discuss replacement pieces or include them in the next sample shipment.

First sample route

Model confirmation → sample invoice → packing → courier pickup → buyer testing.

Bulk-shipment route

Packaging approval → carton quantity → shipping method → invoice and packing list preparation.
