Private Label Checklist for Seed Starter Kits

Plan private-label seed starter kits with model selection, packaging format, barcode, instructions and sample approval.

By MCCS Growing Media Team · 2026-06-04

Private-label seed starter kits involve more than selecting a substrate plug. The pack count, carton quantity, instruction card, label space and channel requirements all affect the final quotation.

Checklist for buyers

  • Target channel: e-commerce, retail store, distributor or nursery customer.
  • Selected CF model and estimated plug count per pack.
  • Packaging format: box, pouch, refill pack or mixed kit.
  • Brand name, barcode, language and warning-label requirements.
  • Instruction-card content and use method.
  • MOQ expectation and outer-carton limitations.

A simple mockup can help confirm the product direction before final artwork. This reduces revisions and helps the supplier quote a more realistic packaging plan.
