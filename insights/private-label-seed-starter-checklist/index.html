Private Label Checklist for Seed Starter Kits

Packaging, labeling and product-selection checklist for private-label seed starter projects.

June 4, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 5 min read

Private-label seed starter kits require more than a plug selection. Packaging format, plug count, instruction content and carton planning all affect cost and buyer experience.

Checklist before artwork

  • Selected CF models and target application.
  • Plug count per retail pack or refill pouch.
  • Instruction-card language and user steps.
  • Barcode, carton markings and outer carton size.
  • Sample approval method before printed packaging.

Why packaging approval matters

A physical sample pack can reveal whether plugs shift during courier delivery, whether instructions are clear and whether the retail box size is practical for the target channel.
