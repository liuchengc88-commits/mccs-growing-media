Private Label Checklist for Seed Starter Kits

Use this private-label checklist to plan packaging, labeling and product selection for a seed starter kit project.

Private-label projects move faster when the buyer prepares the right information before discussing artwork or shipment. Even if the plug itself is already selected, the final offer still depends on packaging and presentation details.

Checklist items to prepare

  • Target sales channel: Amazon, Shopify, distributor or retail store.
  • Selected CF model(s) and estimated plug count per pack.
  • Retail packaging format: box, pouch, refill pack or mixed kit.
  • Brand name, language requirements and barcode information.
  • Instruction card content or hydration guidance.
  • MOQ expectations and bulk packing direction.

It is often better to confirm a simple mockup first, then refine the graphics after sample approval. This reduces back-and-forth and helps both buyer and supplier stay aligned on the real commercial goal.
