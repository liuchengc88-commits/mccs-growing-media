How to Choose the Right Substrate Plug by Application

Choose substrate plugs for seedling, hydroponic, orchid, tissue-culture and succulent programs using application, size and container fit.

By MCCS Growing Media Team · 2026-06-04

Choosing a substrate plug starts with the growing application. A plug for a compact hydroponic planter has different priorities than a plug for orchid transplanting or decorative succulent planting.

For seedling propagation, buyers usually focus on clean handling, tray fit and reliable hydration. For orchid and tissue-culture programs, structure stability and stage matching become more important. For succulent products, visual form and container compatibility are often part of the selling point.

Start with the application

  • Seedling: focus on tray fit, uniformity and handling efficiency.
  • Hydroponic: check holder diameter, plug height and planter compatibility.
  • Orchid: match the plug or cup to the growth stage and pot size.
  • Tissue culture: prioritize uniform size and gentle transition from lab to nursery.
  • Succulent: consider display format, container style and retail presentation.

Then confirm size and packaging

After the application is clear, review the size field and tray-fit note. The next step is packaging: bulk carton, retail box, refill pouch or starter kit. A sample pack across several candidate models is often the fastest way to reduce risk.
