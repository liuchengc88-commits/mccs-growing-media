How to Choose the Right Substrate Plug by Application

Application-based selection logic for seedling, hydroponic, orchid, tissue-culture and succulent programs.

June 4, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 5 min read

Choosing a substrate plug is not simply a matter of size. In B2B sourcing, the right model must match the buyer’s tray, crop stage, packaging channel and expected handling process.

The most reliable starting point is the application. Hydroponic and home-growing programs usually require predictable holder fit and easy insertion. Seedling nurseries care about tray density, handling speed and root development. Orchid and tissue-culture buyers often focus on stage-based transplanting, stable form and compatibility with specialized trays.

Application-first selection

For hydroponic programs, CF-001 and CF-005 are useful starting points. For seedling programs, CF-072, CF-010 and CF-200 cover different tray-density needs. For orchid buyers, CF-015, CF-017, CF-025, CF-028 and CF-035 cover progressive plant stages. For succulent retail and decorative products, CF-004, CF-067 and CF-901/902/903 cover plug, cup and mounting-ball formats.

What to confirm before ordering

After the application is selected, buyers should confirm actual tray opening, wet plug size and packaging method. The listed size is the wet, expanded size because that is the most relevant measurement for tray fit.
