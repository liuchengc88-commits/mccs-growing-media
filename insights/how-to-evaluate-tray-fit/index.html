How to Evaluate Tray Fit and Plug Size

Tray opening, plug height and holder compatibility considerations for B2B sourcing.

June 4, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

Tray fit is one of the most important details in substrate plug sourcing. Even when a plug looks suitable in a photo, a small mismatch in top diameter, bottom diameter or height can create handling problems.

Tray-fit notes should be used as a starting point rather than a substitute for real sample testing. Tray suppliers, hydroponic system manufacturers and garden kit brands may all use different holder dimensions.

Three dimensions to review

Review the top size, bottom size and height together. A plug may sit well at the top but still be too deep, too loose or too tight at the base.

Confirm with real samples

Buyers should test plug insertion, removal, hydration and plant handling inside the actual tray or holder used in production or retail packaging.
