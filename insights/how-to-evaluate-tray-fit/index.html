How to Evaluate Tray Fit and Plug Size

A practical guide to evaluating tray fit, plug size and container compatibility before ordering substrate plugs.

Tray fit is one of the most underestimated details in product sourcing. A visually good plug can still create production issues if it does not sit correctly in the buyer’s tray, pot or hydroponic holder.

That is why product pages should never show dimensions without explaining the intended tray or container fit. In your updated catalog, every product now includes a tray-fit note. This helps the buyer quickly see whether the model is meant for a standard tray, a decorative container or a non-standard home system.

Three checks every buyer should make

  1. Opening size: confirm the holder or tray opening matches the plug’s top and bottom shape.
  2. Plug height: make sure the product is not too shallow or too tall for the intended process.
  3. Workflow: assess whether the plug is being used for propagation, transplanting, display or retail presentation.

When in doubt, do not guess. Ask for a sample pack and test in the actual tray or container that will be used in production or retail packing.
