How to Evaluate Tray Fit and Plug Size

Learn how tray opening, plug height and container fit affect substrate plug selection for B2B orders.

By MCCS Growing Media Team · 2026-06-04

Tray fit is one of the most important details in substrate plug sourcing. A plug may look suitable in a product photo, but if it sits too high, too low or too loosely in the buyer’s tray, it can create production problems.

Buyers should evaluate the opening size, plug height and workflow. The tray-fit field in the CF Series catalog helps clarify whether a model is intended for standard propagation trays, home hydroponic systems, orchid pots or decorative containers.

Three checks before sampling

  1. Opening size: compare the tray or holder opening with the plug’s top and base dimensions.
  2. Plug height: check whether the plug depth is suitable for watering, rooting and transplant handling.
  3. Workflow: decide whether the plug will be used for propagation, display, transplanting or retail packaging.

For the most accurate confirmation, test the sample in the actual tray, pot or holder used in your program.
