How to Evaluate Tray Fit and Plug Size

How tray opening, plug height and container compatibility affect sourcing decisions.

April 27, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

Tray fit determines whether a plug works smoothly in production. Buyers should confirm opening diameter, plug height, expansion after hydration and handling workflow using the real tray or holder before a bulk purchase.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm application and target tray or holder.
  • Request wet-size data and sample testing before bulk order.
  • Clarify packaging format, carton quantity and monthly volume.
  • Discuss SGS test report and export documents if required by your market.
