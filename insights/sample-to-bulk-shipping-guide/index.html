From Sample to Bulk: A Practical Shipping Guide

Move from first samples to trial orders and bulk shipping with a practical substrate plug sourcing workflow.

By MCCS Growing Media Team · 2026-06-04

Most B2B orders begin with samples, but the goal is a repeatable supply plan. A structured sample-to-bulk process reduces risk and makes pricing, packaging and shipping easier to confirm.

A practical sequence

  1. Sample pack: confirm product shape, size and first handling impression.
  2. Trial order: test hydration, tray fit, packing method and basic user feedback.
  3. Packaging confirmation: finalize carton quantity, label requirements and instruction materials.
  4. Bulk shipment: confirm route, documents, carton markings and production schedule.

This process helps buyers and suppliers align on cost, lead time and quality expectations before recurring orders begin.
