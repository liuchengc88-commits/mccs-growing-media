From Sample to Bulk: A Practical Shipping Guide

Understand how buyers can move from first samples to a workable bulk-shipping plan for substrate plug sourcing.

Many inquiries begin with a simple question: “Can you send samples?” But in B2B purchasing, the real goal is to move smoothly from a small evaluation order to a repeatable bulk-shipping structure.

A good shipping discussion should therefore be staged. For the first step, courier delivery is usually the easiest. For the second step, the buyer may test a larger trial quantity. Only after these stages should sea or regular air-shipment planning be discussed.

A practical sequence

  1. Sample pack: verify product size, structure and first impression.
  2. Trial order: test actual use, hydration and basic handling.
  3. Packaging confirmation: finalize carton count, labels and brand presentation.
  4. Bulk shipment: choose the route and confirm supporting documents.

This staged process reduces risk and creates a more professional communication flow for both sides.
