From Sample to Bulk: A Practical Shipping Guide

A staged sourcing workflow from first sample to regular bulk shipment.

May 11, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

A strong sourcing process starts with small samples, moves to trial orders and only then finalizes carton quantity, packaging and shipping route. This staged process reduces risk for both buyer and supplier.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm application and target tray or holder.
  • Request wet-size data and sample testing before bulk order.
  • Clarify packaging format, carton quantity and monthly volume.
  • Discuss SGS test report and export documents if required by your market.
