From Sample to Bulk: A Practical Shipping Guide

Sample-pack, trial-order and bulk-shipment planning for substrate plug sourcing.

June 4, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 5 min read

Many substrate plug projects begin with a small sample request, but the commercial goal is usually repeat supply. A clear shipping plan helps buyers move from first review to trial order and then to a stable bulk-order workflow.

Stage 1: Samples

Samples are used to check size, material feel, hydration, tray fit and first impression. Sample preparation usually takes 3–5 working days after model selection and sample count are confirmed.

Stage 2: Trial order

A trial order gives the buyer enough pieces to test real handling, planting, retail packing and carton performance.

Stage 3: Bulk shipment

Bulk shipment should be planned after product and packaging are approved. Carton quantity is not fixed by model alone; it changes with carton size, retail packaging, private-label inserts and shipment method.
