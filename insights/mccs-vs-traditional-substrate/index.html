MCCS vs Traditional Loose Substrate

Why molded substrate plugs can improve handling, packing and retail presentation.

May 25, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

Loose substrates can be flexible, but they may create handling mess, inconsistent portion control and extra packaging complexity. Molded plugs give B2B buyers a more standardized unit for trays, kits and retail refill packs.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm application and target tray or holder.
  • Request wet-size data and sample testing before bulk order.
  • Clarify packaging format, carton quantity and monthly volume.
  • Discuss SGS test report and export documents if required by your market.
