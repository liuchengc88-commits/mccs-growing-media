Why MCCS

A Net-Free Tapered Plug Alternative for Modern Growing Kits

MCCS is positioned for buyers who need a differentiated growing media plug, not just another loose substrate. The focus is clean handling, compact packing, application-based model selection and export-ready private-label cooperation.

Tall Tapered Root Zone

The 57–65 mm height reference gives buyers a deeper plug body and a stronger visual difference for seed starter kits, nursery trays and hydroponic starting.

Net-Free Molded Structure

No mesh bag is required. The molded structure helps simplify packing and creates a cleaner experience for retail users and professional propagation teams.

Compact for Export & FBA

Dry plugs are compact and easy to carton-pack, making them suitable for distributors, Amazon sellers, Shopify brands and seasonal gardening promotions.

Private Label Ready

Retail boxes, carton labels, instruction cards, barcodes, refill packs and kit combinations can be prepared for overseas buyers.

Batch Testing Support

pH, EC, moisture and formula-related values can be checked according to SKU, batch and buyer requirements before bulk cooperation.

Flexible Market Positioning

MCCS can be positioned between low-cost pellets and premium professional media depending on model, packaging, quantity and market channel.
