Buyer Pain Points

MCCS vs. Traditional Media & Rockwool

Position MCCS as a cleaner, retail-friendly and export-friendly option for buyers who want better user experience than loose substrates and easier handling than traditional propagation blocks.

MCCS Molded Substrate Plugs

  • Pre-molded structure, less messy handling
  • Compact volume for export and FBA storage
  • Good root penetration and transplant handling
  • Private-label retail packaging available
  • Suitable for kits, nurseries and e-commerce listings

Loose Coir / Soil Mix

  • Requires soaking, mixing and cleanup
  • More loose particles and dust during operation
  • Less consistent portion control for retail users
  • Higher packaging mess risk
  • Less suitable for compact kit positioning

Rockwool

  • Professional but often less home-user friendly
  • May require careful pH conditioning by growers
  • Mineral fiber handling can concern retail users
  • Lower natural/eco-friendly story for consumer channels
  • Higher education barrier for beginner kits
