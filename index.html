Comparison

MCCS vs. Loose Media & Rockwool

A cleaner, retail-friendly and export-friendly option for buyers who need easy handling and compact storage.

MCCS Substrate Plugs

  • Pre-molded shape for less mess
  • Compact volume for export and FBA
  • Good root penetration and transplant handling
  • Private-label retail packaging available

Loose Coir / Soil Mix

  • Requires soaking, mixing and cleanup
  • More loose particles and dust
  • Less consistent portion control
  • Higher packaging mess risk

Rockwool

  • Less beginner-friendly
  • Often requires careful pH conditioning
  • Mineral fiber handling may concern retail users
  • Weaker natural-product story
