Buyer FAQ

What Overseas Buyers Usually Need to Confirm

Common questions from distributors, nurseries and retail kit buyers when evaluating molded substrate plugs.

What is the MOQ?

Trial orders are available. Bulk MOQ depends on the selected model, carton size, retail packaging and shipping method.

Are samples free?

Samples may be free or charged depending on model count and quantity. Courier freight is normally paid by the buyer or shipped under the buyer’s account.

How long does sample delivery take?

Sample preparation usually takes 3–5 working days after model confirmation. Courier delivery is commonly 5–9 working days, depending on destination and customs process.

Can packaging be customized?

Bulk carton, retail box, refill pouch, instruction card and label options can be discussed after sample approval.

Which documents can be supported?

Invoice, packing list, SGS test report and route-specific document support can be discussed based on the destination market.

How is carton quantity confirmed?

Carton quantity is a reference only and changes with outer-carton size, retail pack, private-label inserts and shipping method.
