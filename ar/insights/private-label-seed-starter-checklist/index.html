Private Label Checklist for Seed Starter Kits

A B2B checklist for packaging, labels, instructions and sample approval.

May 4, 2026 · MCCS Growing Media · 4 min read

Private-label projects move faster when buyers prepare the target sales channel, plug count, artwork direction, barcode plan and instruction-card requirements before sampling. Sample approval should confirm both product performance and retail presentation.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm application and target tray or holder.
  • Request wet-size data and sample testing before bulk order.
  • Clarify packaging format, carton quantity and monthly volume.
  • Discuss SGS test report and export documents if required by your market.
