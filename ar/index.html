أسئلة المشترين التجاريين

What is MCCS made from?

MCCS products use molded coconut coir and peat substrate with adjustable plant-fiber binder ratios for different applications.

Can you customize the mold?

Yes. Custom mold design can be discussed for qualified B2B projects with tray, cup or retail packaging requirements.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ depends on model, carton size, retail packaging and shipping method. Trial orders are available for qualified B2B buyers.

Can you provide SGS documents?

SGS test report and batch testing information can be shared with eligible buyers during project review.

How long is sample preparation?

Standard sample preparation is usually 3–7 business days after model confirmation.

Do you support private label packaging?

Yes. Retail box, refill pouch, carton markings and instruction cards can be developed after product selection.
