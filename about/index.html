Market Focus

We serve distributors, hydroponic brands, orchid growers, tissue-culture buyers, garden centers and private-label retail kit companies.

Typical destination inquiries include North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific markets.

Testing & Documentation

SGS test report can be shared with qualified buyers upon request. Commercial invoices, packing lists and route-based document support are prepared according to the final shipping plan.

Facility & Capacity

Production and packing capacity is confirmed by model, packaging format and order schedule. For planning purposes, buyers can start with sample packs, then confirm carton quantity and recurring supply volume after testing.
