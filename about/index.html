About MCCS Growing Media

MCCS Growing Media supports overseas buyers with molded substrate plugs, application-based model selection, product testing and overseas order management through Guangzhou Chengfeng Trading Co., Ltd.

Company Profile

Guangzhou Chengfeng Trading Co., Ltd. coordinates inquiries, sample follow-up, packaging discussion and export-facing communication for MCCS Growing Media products.

Location: Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Product range: 19 CF Series molded substrate plug models.

Market Focus

We serve distributors, nurseries, hydroponic brands, orchid growers, tissue-culture buyers, succulent retailers and private-label garden kit companies.

Channels: wholesale distribution, retail kits, refill packs and commercial growing programs.

Testing & Documentation

SGS test report, invoice, packing list and route-based document support can be discussed according to order stage and destination market requirements.

Typical checks: size, tray fit, hydration, handling, packaging and carton configuration.

Facility & Capacity

Model selection, sample packing and bulk carton planning are coordinated through a structured supply chain. Final production capacity is confirmed by selected model, packaging format and order schedule.

Packaging formats: bulk carton, retail box, refill pouch and private-label carton.
