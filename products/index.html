Core B2B Information

Material: Molded coconut coir and peat substrate with adjustable formulation.

Custom formulation: Coco coir, peat moss and binder ratio can be discussed by crop, tray and packaging plan.

Carton quantity: Reference only. Final quantity per carton may change by outer-carton size, retail packaging and shipping method.

Testing: SGS test report, batch testing and published Agronomy 2025 PFAL research references can be discussed for eligible B2B projects.

Technical disclosure: Density, AFP, WHC, EC, pH, ion levels and handling performance are batch- or project-specific unless an approved source states otherwise. Review the technical data matrix.

Research signal: In a lettuce and pak choi PFAL study, molded coconut coir substrate maintained structure during cultivation, reduced exposed roots at transplanting and lowered energy use per unit yield under the test conditions.

Expert Tips

Expert Tips by Application

Seedling

Match plug height with tray depth to reduce handling damage during transplanting.

Hydroponic

Confirm holder diameter, hydration expansion, root containment and PFAL/greenhouse trial goals before bulk ordering.

Orchid

Choose plug size by growth stage, from rooting to transplant cups.

Tissue Culture

Prioritize uniform size and clean handling for post-lab rooting.

Succulent

Use mounting balls and cups for decorative displays, gift sets and specialty retail.
