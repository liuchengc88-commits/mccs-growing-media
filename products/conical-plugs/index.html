Machinery Compatibility

Validate Tray Fit and Automated Handling Before Scale-Up

MCCS supports technical sample review for commercial propagation workflows. Compatibility is confirmed against the buyer's equipment and moisture window, not assumed from the product family name.

Tray and Cavity Fit

Submit cavity width, depth, taper, drainage opening and dimensional tolerance. Dry and hydrated fit can then be checked against the selected CF model.

Pneumatic Grippers

Provide jaw profile, grip pressure, dwell time, line speed and target moisture. Sample trials can record visible damage, crumble loss and transfer consistency.

Drainage and Air Pruning

Confirm bottom clearance and drainage geometry with the actual tray drawing. Air-pruning behavior requires crop- and system-specific validation.

Inputs needed for an automation compatibility review

  • Tray or holder drawing with cavity tolerances
  • Gripper geometry, pressure and cycle rate
  • Target plug moisture at transfer
  • Irrigation pressure, emitter pattern and cycle time
  • Acceptance criteria for fit, damage and throughput

B2B Technical FAQ

Technical Scenarios Buyers Should Validate

These answers distinguish suggested test conditions from published product claims, helping procurement, growers and equipment teams build a defensible approval protocol.

What is the MCCS rehydration time under 1.2 bar water pressure?

MCCS does not publish one universal rehydration time because model dimensions, formulation, initial moisture, water temperature and emitter coverage change the result. A 1.2 bar test can be included in a sample protocol, with time to full wetting and final wet dimensions recorded for the selected model.

Can MCCS plugs meet a crumble rate below 0.5% under pneumatic grippers?

A universal below-0.5% crumble guarantee is not published. Buyers should provide gripper geometry, pressure, cycle rate and target plug moisture. MCCS can then agree a sample handling test, including the proposed 55-65% moisture window, before a bulk specification is approved.

Will a 10 x 10 cm block avoid permanent wilting for 36-42 hours at 25 C?

No universal 36-42 hour claim is made. Wilting depends on crop stage, root mass, radiation, humidity, airflow, nutrient EC, initial saturation and block geometry. The buyer should validate the selected format under the actual greenhouse climate and irrigation strategy.

Is the top-to-bottom AFP gradient below 4% after saturation?

MCCS does not publish a universal below-4% vertical AFP gradient. AFP results require an agreed saturation, drainage, sampling and calculation method. A top-versus-bottom comparison can be requested for a selected model and formulation.

What are the exact sodium and chloride levels, and is the substrate double buffered?

Exact sodium and chloride ppm values must come from the applicable batch or project report. Buffer or wash treatment is confirmed in the approved quotation or batch specification; it should not be inferred from a generic product-page claim.

Does pH stay within plus or minus 0.2 in pure water with EC 0?

MCCS does not publish a universal plus-or-minus 0.2 drift guarantee. The result depends on water alkalinity, extraction ratio, soak time, temperature and formulation. Buyers can request an agreed pure-water soak protocol with starting and final pH recorded.

Can MCCS be stored for 18 months at 85% relative humidity without mold or expansion?

No universal 18-month claim at 85% relative humidity is published. Storage guidance and any project-specific shelf-life statement should be confirmed for the packaging format, warehouse temperature, humidity control and pallet protection used in the shipment.

Can pallets be stacked 10 layers with less than 1.5% expansion-volume loss?

MCCS does not state a universal 10-layer or below-1.5% retention claim. Pallet configuration, carton strength, compression duration, route climate and product format must be reviewed together. Shipment-specific packing and load plans are coordinated before dispatch.

Does the bottom bevel fit standard 128-cell trays and support air pruning?

Tray standards vary by manufacturer. Buyers should submit the cavity drawing, drainage opening and target plug model. MCCS can check dry fit, hydrated fit, drainage clearance and root-zone handling during sample review before bulk ordering.

How do bulk density and AFP change over three cropping cycles?

No universal three-cycle decay rate is published. Reuse performance depends on crop duration, root loading, sanitation, irrigation chemistry and mechanical handling. Buyers planning reuse should run a multi-cycle trial and define density, AFP and structural-retention checkpoints in advance.
